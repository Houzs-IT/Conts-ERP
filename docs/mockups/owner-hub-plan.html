Property OS · plan for the owner, 2 Nov 2026

Owner Hub: one place for owners, the owner portal and Demo mode

Owner Showcase and Showcase control leave the menu. The owner portal, the sample portal and the showcase become one set of screens under Owners. A Demo mode switch, used like light and dark, gives a separate world of sample data that can never reach a real owner.

Your question

Is the Owners page where owners look at their figures?

No. The two screens you sent are the staff view: the Owners list and an owner's profile, which only management and finance open. An owner never sees them. Owners sign in and land on the Owner portal, a separate, simpler screen with only their own units: payout, statements, reservations and reviews. Today the portal and the Owner Showcase's sample portal are two different builds of that screen. The plan below makes them one.

1 · Menu

Owner Showcase and Showcase control leave the menu

Everything about owners sits under Units → Owners, with three things inside:

  • Owners: the list and profiles you have today.
  • View as owner on every owner's profile: opens that owner's real portal, read-only, exactly as they see it.
  • Owner portal (sample): the same portal screens filled from Demo mode. It is what sales shows a prospective owner, and it replaces the Owner Showcase.

Showcase control goes away completely. Figures are no longer typed over one by one: in Demo mode the screens compute from the demo reservations and reviews, like the real ones do.

2 · Demo mode

A switch beside light and dark

Click the switch in the top bar and the whole system enters Demo mode. A blue band stays across the top so nobody forgets.

  • Real reservations keep arriving from HostPlatform and Channex, in both modes.
  • In Demo mode you add sample reservations, reviews, units and owners freely. They are saved in a separate demo store, never in the real tables.
  • The screens show real plus demo together, so a building can look full for a demonstration.
  • Real owners, statements, payouts, invoices, accounting and the channels never read the demo store, so nothing you add can reach an owner's money or Airbnb.
  • Switching back hides the demo data straight away. A Clear demo button removes it all.
  • Every page and PDF made in Demo mode carries a "Sample data" mark.

3 · Owner portal

One portal, three ways in

The portal is built once and opened three ways:

  • The owner signs in: real data, only their units, nothing demo.
  • Staff click View as owner: the same real screens for that owner, read-only, with a band saying who is being viewed. Each view is recorded in History.
  • Owner portal (sample): the same screens fed from Demo mode, for showing prospective owners. A "Sample data" band stays on.

The owner portal's pages: Overview (payout this month, occupancy, average price, rating), Statements with PDF, Reservations, Reviews, Documents, and Property health.

4 · How it is built

Database, server and screens

Real · schema pos

Unchanged

Reservations, reviews, units, owners, statements and payouts stay where they are. HostPlatform, Channex and staff keep writing here.

Demo · new schema demo

Sample data only

Tables shaped like the real ones: demo.reservations, demo.reviews, demo.units, demo.owners, demo.guests. Ids start with demo-, so they can never collide with real ones.

Combined views

What Demo mode reads

demo.v_reservations = real rows + demo rows, and the same for reviews and units, each row marked real or demo.

PartWhat changesGuard that keeps demo away from real owners

Sample portal/api/owner/* gets a sample variant that builds the same answers from demo.v_* for a chosen demo owner.Every answer from it carries sample: true, and the screen shows the Sample data band whenever that is set.

5 · Order of work

Four steps, each one shipped and checked before the next

  1. Menu and View as owner. Remove the two Showcase items, add View as owner on each profile, and make the sample portal use the real portal screens. About half a day.
  2. Demo store. The demo schema, the combined views and the database rule, tried on staging first. About half a day.
  3. Demo switch and writing. The switch, the blue band, demo reservations and reviews on the Reservations, Calendar and Reviews screens, and Clear demo. About one day.
  4. Sample owner portal. The portal fed from Demo mode, which replaces the Owner Showcase, including statements with the Sample data mark. About half a day.
Before I start

Two choices for you

A. Who may turn Demo mode on? Management only (recommended), or also sales staff.

B. Where does demo data start from? Empty, so you add everything yourself (recommended); or a copy of last month's real reservations with guest names and phone numbers replaced, so the screens look full straight away.
