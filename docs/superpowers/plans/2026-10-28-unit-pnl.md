# Unit Cost-to-Income Report (piece 5) — Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** A Finance page that shows, month by month, what each unit and the whole portfolio earned and spent — every cost line on its own — in two layers (the unit's operating profit, then what the company keeps after the owner's portion), with checks that flag numbers that look wrong and months whose data is incomplete.

**Architecture:** One read-only endpoint runs the owner statement engine (`loadInputs` / `computeMonth`, as `functions/api/portfolio/owner-report.ts` does for one month) for each month in the range and adds what guests paid. Pure functions in `src/lib/unitPnl.ts` turn the rows into P&L lines, layers, percentages, checks and data gaps; the page only draws them. Limits chew sets are one JSON value in the existing `settings` table. No migration.

**Tech Stack:** Property OS (repo root): React + Vite in `src/`, Pages Functions in `functions/`, Postgres schema `pos`; `node --test src/lib/*.test.mjs`; endpoint checks in `scripts/api-checks/`. No chart library in the project: charts are small SVG components reading theme CSS variables (as `src/components/Metric.tsx` `BarTrend`).

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-27-utility-bills-design.md` §7 "Unit cost-to-income analysis", and the spike chew reviewed on 2026-10-27 (`exports/单位收入与费用分析-2026-10-27.html`, local only, real data). chew's rulings: income = what the guest paid (A); every cost counted, line by line; both layers shown; monthly two-layer P&L to see the trend; tables with clear lines, headers and subtotals; negatives in brackets and in the danger colour; colours from the system theme; data owned by other teams (bookings import, cleaning costs) is not chew's part — the report flags gaps, it does not fix them.

## Global Constraints

- Product text in English, British spelling, sentence case. Money in sen; months `YYYY-MM`.
- Units covered: the ones the Overall unit report covers — profit-share (PS) and fixed-rent (LT) units; long-term lets and master leases are counted in the page note, not listed (`owner-report.ts:29-31`).
- **Income (guest paid)** for a unit and month = each counted stay's `reservations.gross_booking_value` spread over its nights, the month's share (same stays as the statement: state `confirmed`, `checked_in`, `checked_out`; channel not `maintenance_block` / `owner_stay`). When a stay has no `gross_booking_value` (0), its statement amount is used and the month is flagged "Guest-paid amount missing".
- **Lines**, in this order (label — source):
  1. Guest paid — above.
  2. Platform fees and taxes — guest paid − the statement's gross (`UnitMonth.gross`). Below zero → shown, and flagged "Payout above guest paid".
  3. Cleaning — statement deduction groups `cleaning_basic` + `cleaning_deep`.
  4. Electricity, 5. Water, 6. Internet, 7. Indah Water — groups `electricity`, `water`, `internet`, `sewerage`.
  8. Other costs — group `additional` (maintenance, laundry, consumables and the rest), 9. Misc charge — group `misc`.
  - **Operating profit (layer 1)** = guest paid − lines 2–9 (equals the statement's GP).
  - 10. Owner's portion — the statement's owner base (PS: the higher of GP × owner % and the guarantee; LT: the fixed rent), as `src/lib/ownerOverall.ts`.
  - **Company keeps (layer 2)** = operating profit − owner's portion (equals the Overall report's gain/loss).
- Every money line also shows **% of guest paid** (1 decimal; "–" when guest paid is 0). Negatives print as `(1,234.56)` in the danger colour, never with a minus sign; zero prints `–`.
- Range: last 3, 6 or 12 complete months, default 6; plus the month in progress, labelled "to date". At most 12 months per request.
- **Checks** (each flag names the rule and the numbers behind it):
  - *Own past*: a cost line per night, or the unit's operating margin, more than 50 % away from the unit's average of the previous three months that have it (needs two of three).
  - *Same building*: a unit's cost per night for a line more than 50 % above the median of the units in its building that month (buildings with at least three units that month).
  - *Limits*: chew's limits — per line, "% of guest paid at most" and/or "RM per night at most"; plus "operating margin at least %". None are set at first; checks run only for limits that are set.
- **Data gaps** (shown on the unit and month, and counted in the header): nights booked but no cleaning cost; nights booked but no electricity bill; no nights booked in a month the unit was available; guest-paid amount missing; payout above guest paid.
- Access: `session.manage` reads; saving limits needs a management role (same rule as `PATCH /api/settings`), with `audit`. Both endpoints go into `scripts/api-checks/rbac-matrix.expected.json`.
- Theme: colours only from CSS variables / `TONE_STYLE`; correct in dark mode; no page scroll at phone width (wide tables scroll in their own box).

## Review Focus

1. Totals tie out: for one month, the page's operating profit and company keeps for every unit equal the Overall unit report's GP and gain/loss to the sen. (Task 2 check.)
2. A stay across a month end puts its guest-paid amount in each month by nights, and the two parts add up to the whole. (Task 1 test.)
3. A loss shows in brackets and in the danger colour everywhere (tables, KPI, chart labels), and never as "-". (Task 1 test for the formatter; Task 3 browser check.)
4. Checks never divide by zero: a unit with no nights or no guest paid gets "–" and no per-night or %-based flag. (Task 1 test.)
5. Twelve months of ~120 units load in one request within the Pages time limit. (Task 2 check against the local demo; note the timing.)

### Task 1: P&L rules (pure functions)

**Files:** Create `src/lib/unitPnl.ts`, test `src/lib/unitPnl.test.mjs`.

**Interfaces — Produces:**
- `LINE_KEYS = ['guestPaid','fees','cleaning','electricity','water','internet','sewerage','other','misc','operating','owner','company'] as const`; `LINE_LABEL`.
- `interface UnitMonthInput { unitId; month; contract: 'LT'|'PS'; guestPaid; guestPaidMissing: boolean; gross; cleaning; electricity; water; internet; sewerage; other; misc; ownerPct; ltGrr; nights; days }` (sen).
- `pnl(i: UnitMonthInput): Record<LineKey, number>` — owner's portion via `overallFigures` from `src/lib/ownerOverall.ts` (reuse, do not copy).
- `sumPnl(rows: Record<LineKey, number>[]): Record<LineKey, number>`; `pctOfIncome(line, total): number | null`.
- `bracketMoney(sen: number): { text: string; negative: boolean }` — `(1,234.56)` / `1,234.56` / `–`.
- `spreadGuestPaid(checkIn, checkOut, total, month): number` — the month's share by nights, last month takes the rounding sen.
- `checks(rows: UnitMonthInput[], buildingOf: Record<string,string>, limits: Limits): Flag[]` and `gaps(rows): Gap[]`; `interface Limits { lines: Partial<Record<LineKey, { pctMax?: number; perNightMax?: number }>>; marginMin?: number }`; `interface Flag { unitId; month; rule: 'own_past'|'building'|'limit'; line: LineKey; value: number; reference: number; text: string }`.
