# Renovation projects: user guide

For Dream Crafter staff: designers and managers.

## Projects list

- The list shows one row per project.
- Use the **Sort** dropdown to pick Newest first, Oldest first, Due soonest, Handover soonest or Building A-Z.
- You can also click a column header to sort by that column. The dropdown does not change when you do this.
- The list shows 50 projects at a time. Use the arrows under the list for the next page.
- The line under the list counts all projects that match your filter and search, on every page.
- Tap a row to open the project.

## Calendar > Deadlines

Deadlines are grouped. Each group lists projects with a date or a waiting task.

- **Late**: the due day has passed.
- **Next 7 days**: due within the next 7 days.
- **In 8–14 days** and **Later**: due further out.
- **Waiting for client**: a 3D or 2D drawing was sent and the client has not approved it yet.
- **Ready to start**: the site measure is done and 3D has not started, or 3D is approved and 2D has not started.
- **To book / to do**: tasks that still need a booking or action. If there are none, you see "Nothing to book or do right now."

Buttons on a waiting row:

- **+ Amendment**: starts an amendment round on the drawing.
- **Client approved**: records that the client approved the drawing.

Use the person picker to see your own deadlines or the whole team.

## Calendar > Progress row steps

Each project has one row of circles. The steps are:

Measure, 3D, 2D, Carpentry, Site PIC, Variants.

Your company may show a slightly different list. The Variants step is always last.

- A filled tick means the step is done.
- The ring means it is the next step to do.
- A step can be done out of order. For example, Variants is done when every sales order line has its variants, even if Site PIC is not done yet.
- Measure, Carpentry and Site PIC count as done once their booked day has passed, or the measure is recorded. Carpentry and Site PIC also count as done when someone ticks them.
- 3D and 2D are done when the client approval is recorded.
- The line between two steps lights up only when both steps are done, or when every step before is done.

## Project page stepper

The stepper at the top of a project shows the main flow:

Sales order, Design, Ordering, Renovation, Handover, Closed.

- Sales order is done once the order is confirmed (not Draft, not Cancelled).
- Design is done when ordering starts.
- Handover shows the handover date when one is set.

Under Renovation there is a second row, one circle per category:

Works, Built-in, Lighting, Service (your categories may differ: they come from the renovation catalog settings).

- A category is done when all its lines are done. The small text shows lines done out of total.
- **Not in SO** (dashed circle) means the sales order has no line in that category.
- Click a circle to read its rule and see what is still open.

## Chart of accounts

The page is headed Finance, Chart of accounts. You need permission to manage it to see the buttons.

- **Add account**: top right of the page. Creates a new account and puts it in every company you tick.
- **Upload AutoCount chart**: top right. Pick an .xlsx or .xls file exported from AutoCount.
- A **tick** in a company column means that company uses the account. Untick it to stop using it.
- Unticking an account also unticks the accounts under it. You get a confirm box first.
- With **Quick mode** on, unticking a single account (one with nothing under it) and deleting skip the confirm box. Unticking a parent account still asks.
- An account with transactions or references cannot be deleted. Untick it instead.
