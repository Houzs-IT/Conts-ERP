## How the local copy was run (for the next sweep)

Property OS on `wrangler pages dev` with `scripts/dev-pglite.mjs`; the engine on `wrangler dev` with its own
`scripts/dev-pglite.mjs` instance, the engine's built screens on `vite preview`, and a local `.dev.vars` pair with a
fresh random single sign-on secret. Two things a next sweep needs to know:

- PGlite answers one connection at a time; under a browser's parallel requests it stalls. A small proxy that lets
  one request at a time through to a single PGlite connection fixed it (not committed; local test tooling only).
- `dev-pglite.mjs` sets the search path to `pos, public` for the rental app. The engine's tables are in `public`, so
  its database needs `set search_path = public, extensions` after start, or engine sign-in fails.
