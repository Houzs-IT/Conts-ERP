# Design System: The Conts ERP

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Private Ledger"**

The Conts reads like a well-kept account book. Warm stone paper, near-black ink, and a single champagne gold that appears only where something belongs to The Conts or is selected. Every screen is a page in that ledger: an owner checking a statement, a supervisor clearing today's turnovers, finance closing a payout run. The design earns trust by being exact, not by being loud.

Density is operational. Tables run at 48px rows (40px compact), body copy sits at 13px, and every number is set in tabular figures so a column never shifts. A dark basalt sidebar frames a light workspace; everything inside the workspace is flat, separated by 1px warm borders rather than shadows. Motion is short (120 to 240ms) and never decorates a number.

The system is token driven by brand rule: `tokens.css` at the repo root is the one file a brand change touches, and many of its values are marked PROVISIONAL until the client supplies final artwork. The supply chain engine (`engine/`) has its own stylesheet and is recoloured to this palette at build time; it is not governed component by component by this file.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Warm neutrals (stone and basalt), never cold greys.
- Champagne gold as identity and selection only, never a status.
- Flat by default: a 1px border and no shadow; shadows only on things that float.
- Status always carries a text label; colour is never the only signal.
- Tabular figures on every money value, date, metric and table.
- One sans family (IBM Plex Sans), mono only for codes and references.

## Colors

A warm monochrome ledger with one metallic accent and five quiet status tones.

### Primary
- **Champagne Gold** (`champagne-gold`): the brand mark. Active nav indicator (2px inset bar), sidebar count dots, focus border, selected chart series, the Direct booking channel, brand buttons' border. Never used as text on light surfaces (it fails contrast); use Gold Ink instead.
- **Gold Ink** (`gold-ink`): the lightest gold that passes AA as text on white. Link hover, accent text, "link action" buttons inside cards, Direct channel labels.
- **Gold Tint** (`gold-tint`): selected table row and Direct channel tint.
- **Gold Highlight** (`gold-highlight`): text selection background.

### Neutral
- **Brand Black** (`brand-black`): the sidebar shell, primary buttons, inverse surfaces, the login stage under the blueprint grid.
- **Basalt Charcoal** (`basalt-charcoal`) and **Basalt Raised** (`basalt-raised`): active nav rows, the workspace switcher track, raised elements on the dark shell.
- **On Dark** (`on-dark`) and **On Dark Muted** (`on-dark-muted`): text on the shell. The muted step (5.04:1) is the quietest allowed; white alpha text is banned on dark.
- **Ink** (`ink`), **Ink Secondary** (`ink-secondary`), **Ink Muted** (`ink-muted`): primary text, secondary text, labels and subtitles.
- **Ink Faint** (`ink-faint`): placeholders and disabled text only, never body copy.
- **Stone Page** (`stone-page`): the workspace background. **Card White** (`card-white`): cards, dialogs, inputs. **Stone Muted** (`stone-muted`): table headers, read-only fields, disabled buttons. **Stone Sunken** (`stone-sunken`): skeletons and inset wells.
- **Line Default** (`line-default`), **Line Strong** (`line-strong`), **Line Hairline** (`line-hairline`): card and input borders, stronger dividers, dense-table hairlines.

### Status tones
Each tone has three roles because one hex cannot do all three jobs: a tint background (`*-bg`), a text colour measured at 4.5:1 or better on that tint (`*-fg`), and a line colour for dots, borders and chart fills (`*-line`). The brand guide's own base values fail AA as 11px badge text, so they live on `-line` and the `-fg` steps are darkened. This correction is flagged for the client.
- **Success** (`success-*`): Ready, Checked in, Resolved, Paid.
- **Warning** (`warning-*`): Pending, Dirty, Waiting.
- **Critical** (`critical-*`): Overdue, No show, Maintenance, destructive buttons (solid `critical-fg` fill with white text).
- **Info** (`info-*`): Confirmed, Occupied, In progress.
- **Neutral** (`neutral-*`): Inquiry, Checked out, Blocked.

### Channel marks
Airbnb (`channel-airbnb`), Booking.com (`channel-booking`) and Agoda (`channel-agoda`) keep their own brand colours, the way a channel manager shows them, with a tint and an AA ink for each in `tokens.css`. They identify third parties and are not part of the palette. Direct uses gold on purpose: it is The Conts's own commission-free channel. Maintenance blocks use a dashed critical border, owner stays a dotted neutral border, so channels stay distinguishable without colour.

### Named Rules
**The Gold Is a Signature Rule.** Gold marks The Conts or the current selection. It never means success, warning or error, and it is never an accepted Badge tone.

**The Three Roles Rule.** Status text uses `-fg` on `-bg`; `-line` is never used for text.

**The Warm Neutral Rule.** Every grey in the product is warm (stone or basalt). Do not introduce cool slate or pure greys.

## Typography

**Body Font:** IBM Plex Sans (fallback ui-sans-serif, system-ui, Segoe UI)
**Mono Font:** IBM Plex Mono (fallback ui-monospace, Cascadia Code)

**Character:** A single engineered sans, used at modest sizes with semibold for hierarchy. It reads like a typeset statement, not a marketing page. Stylistic sets `ss01`, `cv02`, `cv11` are on globally; tables, money and metrics switch on `tnum` and slashed zero.

The shared UI font is still an open client decision (Houzs ships Tahoma, Hookka ships IBM Plex Sans). Changing it means editing `--font-sans` and nothing else.

### Hierarchy
The app uses a six-step scale (UI audit, 8 Oct 2026), exposed as `text-caption` to `text-headline` utilities and in `src/theme.ts`.
- **Display** (600, 40px, 1.1, -0.02em): reserved for brand surfaces; defined in the tokens, not yet used in the app.
- **Headline** (600, 26px, 1.2, -0.01em): the page title in `PageHeader`.
- **Title** (600, 19px, 1.3): section headings within a page.
- **Heading** (600, 15px, 1.35): card titles.
- **Body** (400, 13px, 1.5): table cells, form values, nav items, buttons (buttons at weight 500).
- **Meta** (400, 12px, 1.5): page subtitles (count and period), field labels and help text, badges, sidebar L3 items.
- **Label** (500, 11px, 0.05em, uppercase): the eyebrow above stat values and table column headers. Sidebar group labels are 10px, 600, 0.13em uppercase.
- **Mono** (400, 12px): codes, IDs and references only. Money uses the sans face with tabular figures, prefixed "RM ".

### Named Rules
**The Tabular Figures Rule.** Every financial value, date, metric and table column uses tabular figures, so a number can never reflow a column.

**The Two Uppercases Rule.** Uppercase is reserved for small labels (eyebrows, column headers, nav group labels) and the THE CONTS wordmark (0.18em tracking). Nothing else is set in capitals.

**The 11px Floor Rule.** No authored size below 11px (the 10px nav group label is the single exception, on the dark shell).

Note: root `tokens.css` still lists the brand guide's earlier scale (14px body, 28px page title). The running app follows the six-step scale above; reconcile `tokens.css` when it next changes.

## Layout

A dark fixed sidebar (244px, collapsible to 68px) and a light scrolling workspace capped at 1600px. Page padding is 32px on desktop, 24px on tablet, 16px on phones, on a 12-column grid with 24px gutters. Spacing follows an 8px base with a 4px half step (4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, 32, 40, 48).

Every page opens the same way: `PageHeader` with the title, a one-line subtitle, and actions at the top right in the order [secondary] [secondary] [Primary]; on a phone the action row wraps under the title, left-aligned. Record pages add a small "‹ All owners" back link above the title.

Control heights: 40px default, 36px in toolbars and filter rows, 44px on touch (field crew app, owner portal, and any coarse pointer). Table rows 48px, 40px compact. Sidebar items 34px (L3 children 30px), with L2 indented 12px and L3 32px behind a 1px guide line.

The field crew and owner apps have their own shells with no staff sidebar: a brand black top bar (logo mark, name, log out) over a single column. The field crew column is capped at 560px with 16px padding and 44px targets, built for one-handed use on a phone.

## Elevation & Depth

Flat is the default. Surfaces separate by tone (stone page, white card, stone-muted header) and 1px warm borders, not shadows. Shadows appear only on things that genuinely float above the page.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Card** (`0 1px 2px 0 rgba(21,21,21,0.04), 0 0 0 1px rgba(21,21,21,0.04)`): a whisper of lift, used today only on the reservation calendar grid; ordinary cards use a plain border instead.
- **Raised** (`0 2px 8px 0 rgba(21,21,21,0.06)`): defined in the tokens, not yet used in the app.
- **Dropdown** (`0 4px 6px -1px rgba(21,21,21,0.08), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(21,21,21,0.06)`): menus and select popovers.
- **Overlay** (`0 12px 32px 0 rgba(21,21,21,0.16)`): dialogs and drawers, over a scrim of brand black at 44%.

### Named Rules
**The Flat by Default Rule.** At rest, a surface is a 1px border and no shadow. If it does not float, it does not cast.

## Shapes

Gently rounded, never sharp and never bubbly. Controls (buttons, inputs, selects) use an 8px radius; cards, dialogs and panels 12px; status badges and compact filters are full pills. Small inner elements (sidebar toggles, skeleton blocks) use 4 to 6px. Inputs keep the 8px radius even though the Hookka sibling uses square inputs: the one deliberate divergence from the shared stylesheet.

`tokens.css` defines three textures that carry meaning, not decoration: the blueprint grid (32px, white at 3.5% on brand black) for the sign-in stage and inverted brand surfaces; the stone dot grid (16px) for empty or unconfigured modules; diagonal tape stripes for blocked units and unavailable dates. Today only the sign-in stage uses one, and its plain-black background override currently hides the grid lines.

Icons are lucide-react 0.477.0 only, stroke 1.75: 16px in tables, 17px in the nav, 20px default, 24px for high-level actions.

## Components

Quiet and exact: flat fills, crisp 1px borders, short colour transitions, nothing that bounces.

### Buttons
- **Shape:** gently rounded (8px).
- **Primary:** brand black fill, white 13px medium text, 0 16px padding, 40px tall. One per page, last in the action row.
- **Secondary (default):** white fill, ink text, 1px default border.
- **Tertiary:** transparent, muted ink text, for low-weight actions.
- **Brand:** transparent with a champagne gold border and gold ink text, for The Conts's own premium actions.
- **Danger:** solid critical fill (`critical-fg`) with white text, for destructive confirms.
- **Sizes:** sm 36px (0 13px), md 40px (0 16px), touch 44px (0 18px, 15px text).
- **Disabled:** stone-muted fill, faint text, not-allowed cursor, and a required reason shown as the title. A disabled control always says why.
- **Loading:** a 14px ring spinner replaces the icon; the label stays.
- **Transition:** background and border colour over 120ms. No hover colour change is defined on the shared Button yet.

### Chips / Badges
- **Style:** full pill, tone tint background with `-fg` text, 12px medium (11px for the small size), 4px 10px padding.
- **Rule:** a label is required; tone is one of success, warning, critical, info, neutral. Gold is not a tone.
- **Dot:** a 6px dot in the tone's `-line` colour, always beside a text label. Only a live-connection dot may pulse.

### Cards / Containers
- **Corner Style:** 12px.
- **Background:** white on the stone page.
- **Shadow Strategy:** none at rest; a 1px default border (see Elevation & Depth).
- **Internal Padding:** header 18px 20px 14px with a 15px semibold title and an optional right-side action (a gold ink "link action" or a button).

### Inputs / Fields
- **Style:** white, 1px default border, 8px radius, 40px tall (44px on touch), 0 14px padding, 13px text.
- **Label:** always visible above the field in 12px medium muted ink, 7px gap. A placeholder is an example, never a label.
- **Focus:** border turns champagne gold with a 2px gold ring at 35%.
- **Error:** border in `critical-line`, message below in `critical-fg`. **Read-only:** stone-muted fill, muted text.
- **Numeric:** money and metric inputs are right-aligned with tabular figures.

### Navigation
- **Sidebar:** brand black shell, 244px (68px collapsed). Group labels 10px uppercase; items 34px at 13px in on-dark text. Active item: basalt charcoal fill with a 2px champagne gold inset bar on the left and white text. L3 children sit behind a 1px guide line; the active child gets a 14% gold wash. Counts are small pills (gold on 18% gold when active); collapsed items show a 7px gold dot.
- **Workspace switcher:** a two-segment control on a charcoal track, used where supply chain screens are enabled.
- **Profile menu:** a white popover card with overlay shadow above the sidebar footer.
- **Mobile:** the field crew and owner shells have no navigation menu; a brand black top bar carries the logo mark, the user's name and a 44px log out button.

### Dialogs and Drawers
- **Dialog:** white, 12px radius, overlay shadow, max 440px wide, over a 44% brand black scrim; enters with a short rise and slight scale (240ms).
- **Drawer:** slides from the right, 480px default, full height.

### Status Badge and Channel Mark (signature)
The closed status vocabulary in `copy.md` maps every state to one label and one tone. Channel marks pair each booking source's colour with a glyph and label; non-revenue blocks use dashed or dotted borders so the calendar reads without colour.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** take every colour, radius, shadow and duration from `tokens.css` (or `src/theme.ts`, which mirrors it). Nothing hard-codes a brand hex.
- **Do** use Gold Ink (#8A6A2F) for any gold text on light surfaces; Champagne Gold is for marks, borders and fills.
- **Do** pair every status colour with its text label, using `-fg` on `-bg`.
- **Do** set money, dates, metrics and table columns in tabular figures, with "RM " before amounts.
- **Do** keep surfaces flat with 1px warm borders; reserve shadows for menus, dialogs and drawers.
- **Do** order page actions as [secondary] [secondary] [Primary], with one primary action.
- **Do** use 44px targets on touch surfaces (field crew, owner portal).
- **Do** keep motion between 120 and 240ms with the ease-out curve, and honour reduced motion.

### Don't:
- **Don't** use gold to mean success, warning or error, or as a Badge tone.
- **Don't** put white-alpha text on the dark shell; use On Dark or On Dark Muted.
- **Don't** use a `-line` status colour as text.
- **Don't** introduce cool greys, a second icon family, or a second sans typeface.
- **Don't** animate a financial value or loop any animation except the live-connection dot.
- **Don't** place dense UI text directly on photography; use the photo scrim.
- **Don't** use uppercase outside small labels and the wordmark.
